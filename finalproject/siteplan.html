Site Purpose

Scope of the website's content

The site shows the information about the Software Engineer: their experience, credentials, their projects, and how to contact them to hire them or purchase their services.

Scenarios

  • Who is this Software Engineer?

  • Where can I find their contact information to get in contact with them?
